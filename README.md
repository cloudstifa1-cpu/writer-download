# Fablesmith (test version)

By Immortal Art Studios LLC.

An offline writing app for **books and screenplays**. Your writing stays on your device.

- **Books:** chapters, a vault that links names on its own (with pictures, key facts, connections and a relationship map), Describe later sensory questions, Draft and Revise modes with revision checks, notes to yourself, find and replace, read aloud, focus mode, writing sprints, goals and streaks, an outline board and a story timeline.
- **Screenplays:** type and it's formatted as a script (Tab or the buttons switch between scene heading, action, character and dialogue). Standard pages with (MORE) and (CONT'D), PDF, Final Draft (.fdx) and Fountain.
- **Publishing:** title, copyright and dedication pages, ePub with a cover, print-ready PDF with the wider inside margin Amazon KDP asks for, and the standard manuscript format agents want.
- **Safe:** undo for everything, a Trash, version history for every chapter, daily automatic backups, and syncing between your phone and computer through Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive.
- **Opens** Word, PDF, ePub, text, Markdown, Final Draft, Fountain and Scrivener projects.

## Install

**Windows:** run the installer. Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because this test version isn't signed yet. Click **More info → Run anyway**.

**Mac:** open the .dmg and drag **Fablesmith** into **Applications**. The first time, right-click Fablesmith and choose **Open**, then **Open** again (it isn't signed by Apple yet). On newer macOS you may need **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.

**Android:** tap the download, then tap the file in your notifications or **Files → Downloads**. If asked, allow installing from this source, then tap **Install**.

Fablesmith backs itself up every day you write (Windows and Mac: **Documents › Fablesmith › Backups**; Android: **Downloads › Fablesmith backups**). To write on both your phone and your computer, open **⋯ → Backups and sync**.

Fablesmith checks this page once a day for a new version and tells you when one is out.
